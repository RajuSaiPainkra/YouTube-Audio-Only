# YouTube-Audio-Only Chrome Extension
**Overview:** <br>The YouTube-Audio-Only Chrome extension provides a unique browsing experience for YouTube users by allowing them to play only the audio of YouTube videos, saving bandwidth and data usage. Additionally, it offers a convenient option to download the audio of the video for offline listening. This extension is particularly useful for users who want to enjoy music or podcasts from YouTube without consuming unnecessary data or resources associated with video playback.<br>


<hr>

### Features:
<ol>
<li>Play Audio Only:</li>
<ul>
    <li>Stops the video from playing and mutes it while still playing the audio in the background.</li>
    <li>Ideal for users who are interested in the audio content, such as music, podcasts, or interviews, without the need for video.</li>
</ul>
<li>Audio Download:</li>
<ul>
    <li>Allows users to download the audio from YouTube videos in a simple, straightforward format.</li>
    <li>Enables offline listening to your favorite audio content directly from YouTube.</li>
</ul>

<li> User-friendly Interface:</li>
<ul>
    <li>The extension provides an easy-to-use interface with a popup menu containing two simple buttons:<li>
    <ul><li> Play Audio Only:<br> Stops video playback and mutes the video while maintaining the audio.</li>
        <li>Download Audio: <br> Downloads the audio in a format that can be stored and listened to offline.</li>
    </ul>
</ul>

<li>Efficient Data Usage:</li>
    <ul><li>By disabling the video playback, the extension helps users save valuable data, especially when on mobile data or in areas with limited bandwidth.</li></ul>

<li>Seamless Integration with YouTube:</li>
    <ul><li>The extension integrates smoothly into the YouTube website, making it easy for users to control their audio experience without any interruptions.</li></ul>

</ol>
<hr>

### Technologies Used:
<ul>
    <li>Chrome Extension API: <br> For interacting with the browser and manipulating YouTube pages.</li>
    <li>JavaScript: <br>For handling the logic behind toggling the audio and video, and downloading the audio file.</li>
    <li>HTML/CSS: <br>For creating the extension's popup interface and styling.</li>

<hr>

### How It Works:
<ol>
<li>Once installed, the extension adds two buttons to the YouTube video page:</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Play Audio Only: <br> When clicked, it stops the video playback and mutes the video while continuing to play the audio.</li>
        <li>Download Audio:<br> When clicked, the audio of the current YouTube video is downloaded to your device in MP4 format.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>The extension modifies the video element on the YouTube page to play only the audio, thus saving data and system resources.</li>
</ol>

<hr>

### Use Case:

<ul>
    <li>Music Enthusiasts: <br> Users can listen to their favorite music on YouTube without video playback.</li>
    <li>Podcasts:<br> Ideal for users who prefer listening to podcasts on YouTube without consuming excess data.</li>
    <li>Data-Saving Mode:<br> Perfect for users with limited internet bandwidth or when using mobile data.</li>


<hr>

### Getting Started:
<ol>
    <li>Install the YouTube-Audio-Only Chrome extension from the Chrome Web Store.</li>
    <li>Click on the extension icon when on a YouTube page to open the popup interface.</li>
    <li>Click the Play Audio Only button to stop the video and play the audio.</li>
    <li>Click the Download Audio button to save the audio to your device for offline use.</li>
</ol>
